FMTRX player invitation status compatibility audit — 2026-10-16

Scope
Existing fungoweb/main; branch confirmed and working tree initially clean.
Production counts (2,206 players; 2,176 status=1; 30 status=0) were supplied
by the user, not independently queried. No production access or changes.

Findings
1. The original users migration defaults status to false. AuthUtils::saveUser
   does not set it, including in the initial repository commit 34bb6a6.
   RegisterPlayerController saves the profile and issues an authentication
   token without activation. AuthUtils::authCredentials does not filter status.
   Thus status=0 is compatible with a legitimate registered player account.
2. CoachUtils::saveNewUser explicitly writes status=true for coach-created
   players without passwords. The normal new unclaimed roster-player path
   produces status=1, not 0. There is no evidence here that production's 30
   status=0 accounts are all unclaimed or all inactive. Their origin cannot be
   determined from aggregate counts alone.
3. CompletePlayerController sets email/password/type without changing status.
   Status=1 therefore does not prove claimed identity or verified email, and
   status=0 is not a reliable account-suspension marker.
4. AccountDeletionService sets status=false AND anonymizes the account,
   revokes credentials/removes relationships and soft-deletes User. The
   default User query scope excludes deleted records. This remains enforced.
5. The invitation system introduced player-status predicates in its list,
   issuance, queued delivery/resend eligibility and acceptance. These could
   reject legitimate status=0 players independently of actual eligibility.

Smallest correction
Removed only player-recipient status predicates from InvitationController,
InvitationService and InvitationEligibility. No account status is written.
Coach-recipient checks and the prior acting-coach authorization are unchanged.
Invitation listings retain authorized coach and active team relationship
filters. They list candidates; issuance separately enforces age eligibility.

Security preserved
Nondeleted existing User, player role, authorized coach, active player-team
membership, confirmed age >=13, matching recipient email, single-use expiring
link/code, and existing-account ownership proof remain required. Unknown,
invalid/future and under-13 ages remain blocked. Acceptance cannot create a
new player or resurrect a removed relationship. Email verification remains
recorded on the accepted invitation. Delivery switches and allowlists remain
unchanged; tests use mail, notification and queue fakes.

Scope boundary
Legacy PlayerProfileClaimService team/individual-code flows still require
status=1. Those separate claim paths were not relaxed. Their normal unclaimed
coach-created records already have status=1. A future compatibility audit may
consider exceptional status=0 placeholders there; this change is limited to
secure email invitations. No general notification status filters were changed.
